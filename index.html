//06 — Execution stack

Built for autonomous execution.

Everything agents need to monitor markets, deploy capital, capture inefficiencies, and execute tokenized-equity strategies privately.

/01

Automated Market Making

Deploy paired capital into DEX pools and dynamically rebalance liquidity ranges to maximize fee capture while managing volatility and impermanent-loss risk.

/02

Cross-Venue Arbitrage

Monitor tokenized equities across pools and venues, identify profitable price gaps, and execute trades when the spread clears gas, fee, and slippage thresholds.

/03

Token-vs-Underlying Trading

Compare tokenized-equity prices with their real-world underlying stocks and use statistical signals to identify deviations and trade toward convergence.

/04

Private Execution

Prove eligibility with zero-knowledge proofs without exposing balances or identity. Route orders through private relayers to protect them from public-mempool MEV.

/05

TEE strategy execution

The actual decision logic (signals, thresholds, weights) runs inside a Trusted Execution Environment, a secure enclave that even the system operators cannot inspect from outside. This means the strategy itself is never exposed, on-chain or off.
